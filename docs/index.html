A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your first creation.

What can I create?

Athlete Spotlight, Game Day, Milestones, Recruiting, Birth Announcements, Graduation, Birthday, Wedding and All Occasions artwork. Each category has multiple styles and a form for its specific details.

Will my artwork look exactly like the sample?

Samples show the direction of each style. Your artwork is generated using your photos and details, so results vary. AI may make mistakes in likeness, spelling or other details; always review your graphic before sharing.

What happens to unused credits?

Unused trial credits expire at the end of the trial. Monthly credits expire at renewal. Purchased refill credits carry over indefinitely and remain in your account after cancellation. Active subscription access is required to generate, and monthly credits are used before purchased credits.

Where is GameVida available?

GameVida is available now for Android in the United States. Download it from Google Play. We are focusing on Android first.

How are my photos and account handled?

Photos and details needed for generation are sent to our secure backend and AI provider. Read our privacy policy for processing, storage and deletion information. Account deletion is available through the app and the account-deletion instructions linked in the policy.
