GameVida Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

GameVida creates personalized artwork from photos, logos and details you provide. This policy explains how GameVida handles information when you browse artwork styles, sign in, create graphics, make purchases, save or share images, or request account deletion.

Information we process

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier and authentication information. If you choose Google sign-in, our authentication provider receives the basic profile information Google supplies, such as your name and profile image.
  • Artwork content: selected photos and logos, names, dates, schools, teams, captions and other details you enter, along with the resulting artwork. Only upload content you have the right to use.
  • Purchases and credits: subscription and refill purchase records, purchase tokens, order identifiers, subscription status and credit transactions. Google Play handles payment methods; GameVida does not receive or store your full card number or card security code.
  • Service activity: generation requests and their status, usage and cost records, artwork reports and operational diagnostics. Our service providers also receive technical request information, such as IP addresses, timestamps and error or status information.
  • Support correspondence: messages you send to GameVida support, your reply address and any attachments you choose to include.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, create and display artwork, validate purchases, maintain credits, prevent duplicate charges and misuse, process artwork reports, recover interrupted requests, provide account and recovery emails, respond to support requests, diagnose service problems and honor deletion requests. We do not display targeted advertising or sell your personal information.

AI processing and service providers

When you request a graphic, your selected reference images and artwork details are sent through GameVida's secure backend to OpenAI for image generation. Generated text and likenesses can contain errors; review the result before using or sharing it. Your original files on your device are not modified by generation.

GameVida uses service providers including Supabase for authentication and database services, Railway for backend hosting, OpenAI for image processing, Resend for account email and support correspondence, and Google for sign-in and Play billing. Each receives information needed for its role. These services have their own security, retention and legal obligations. OpenAI's API handling and retention practices are described in its data controls documentation. Do not assume that provider processing is instantaneous or that all provider records disappear when you close a screen.

Server storage and device copies

Account records, generated artwork, purchase and credit records, generation usage and artwork reports are stored in GameVida's server database. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not, by itself, delete your server account or its records.

Previews may be cached temporarily on your device. Choosing Save creates a separate device copy; local albums and saved or shared images may remain after you sign out or delete your GameVida account. Keep your own copies of artwork you want to retain. GameVida is not a guarantee of permanent backup or cross-device album synchronization.

Photos, permissions and sharing

Selected reference images may be resized or compressed on your device to prepare a reliable upload. This does not change the original files in your photo library. Uploaded logos retain transparency where supported.

GameVida uses the system picker to let you select reference images. Saving artwork may require the operating system's media-saving permission. You can manage applicable permissions in device settings.

Sharing a completed graphic is optional and initiated by you. Your selected destination app receives the image and any text you choose to share, and that service's policies apply. GameVida does not automatically publish posts, send messages to coaches or notify social accounts. There is no public profile or in-app social feed.

Subscriptions and retention

Google Play manages subscription payments, cancellation and refunds. We retain purchase and credit records to provide your entitlement and handle restoration, renewals and refunds. Subscription cancellation does not delete your account. Purchased credits remain associated with your account after cancellation, although an active subscription is required to generate artwork.

Generated artwork and account-associated records remain in our active database until your account and data are deleted, subject to service operation and applicable legal obligations. Technical logs and provider backups may remain for their applicable retention periods. Provider-held records and payment records remain subject to the relevant provider's policies and obligations.

Support messages are stored separately in our email service to respond to inquiries and maintain necessary correspondence. Deleting your app account does not automatically remove those messages. Contact support if you want to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion from Subscription & credits → Delete account in the app, or through the GameVida account-deletion page without installing the app. Sign in to the account you want to delete and follow the confirmation steps. A recent sign-in is required to protect your account.

Completed deletion removes your GameVida identity and account-associated generated artwork, credit balances, purchase records, usage records and artwork reports from our active app database. Unused credits are forfeited. If a graphic is still being created, wait for it to finish before submitting the deletion request. Accepted requests are retried if a provider is unavailable. A limited deletion-status marker is retained for up to 24 hours after completion to prevent stale requests from recreating deleted data, then removed.

Deleting your GameVida account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in Google Play subscriptions. Copies you saved or shared, and records retained independently by Google or other providers, must be managed through those services.

Security

GameVida uses encrypted connections for account, artwork and purchase requests. Server credentials remain on the backend, and access to account artwork and credit records requires authentication. No service can guarantee complete security. Protect your device and sign-in credentials, and avoid uploading information you do not want processed for artwork generation.

Children and other people's information

GameVida is not designed for children to use independently. If you provide information or images of other people, including children, you must have appropriate permission. Contact us if you believe information has been submitted without that permission. GameVida does not offer public profiles, a social feed or automatic publication of family content.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact support@gamevida.app. Please do not include passwords, payment card details or sensitive images in an initial message. Use the account-deletion form for an authenticated deletion request.

We may update this policy when the service or its practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
